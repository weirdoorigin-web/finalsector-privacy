Final Sector – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Final Sector racing game ("the game", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The game is published on Google Play by The Chapter Code (package name com.finalsector.racing).

Summary

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • The game shows no ads and contains no in-app purchases.
  • The game does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not collect your real name, email address, contacts, location, photos or device identifiers such as the advertising ID.
  • For online races, a random player ID and the nickname you choose are sent to our multiplayer service so other players can race with you.

Information the game uses

Player ID. When the game is first started it creates a random identifier for that installation. It is not linked to your Google account, phone number or hardware identifiers. It is used to recognise your car in online sessions and keep matchmaking working. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data creates a new one.

Nickname. You can choose a driver nickname, otherwise a generated name such as "Driver1234" is used. It is shown to other players in online races and leaderboards inside the session. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as your nickname.

Gameplay data during online races. While you race online, your car's position, speed, race progress and pit-stop actions are exchanged with the other players in the same session in real time. This data is not stored after the session ends.

Data stored on your device. Settings, progress, garage and unlocked items are saved locally on your device only.

Third-party services

Online multiplayer is provided by Photon (Exit Games GmbH), which relays session data between players and, like any internet service, processes your IP address to connect you. Photon acts as a service provider for the game. See the Photon privacy policy at photonengine.com/privacy.

The game is distributed through Google Play, which may collect information under Google's own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with third parties for advertising or any other purpose. Your nickname is visible to the other players in the same online session.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Retention and deletion

We do not keep a server-side profile of you. Online session data exists only for the duration of a session. To remove the player ID, nickname and progress stored on your device, clear the game's data or uninstall it. If you have questions or want help with your data, contact us at the address below.

Security

We collect as little information as possible. No sensitive personal information is collected.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
